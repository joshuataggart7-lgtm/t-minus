T-Minus. Mission Acquisition Acceleration. Procurement at the Speed of Mission.

WHAT THIS IS
T-Minus turns acquisition time into mission readiness. Procurement has been the invisible critical path that makes missions late. T-Minus makes it visible: for every priority project, leaders see the acquisition phase and next decision, days to that decision and to award, a status of On Track / Needs Attention / At Risk, the critical-path blocker and who owns it, time saved or schedule impact against the mission date, and one line on what leadership needs to know now. Underneath, an acquisition is entered once and every document, check, and record derives from that record. A missing document or open red flag puts it on hold with the reason and an owner. Reviews are a go/no-go poll where each required reviewer votes Go or No-go by name. Award reads Launched. Word/Excel templates become versioned HTML forms. FAR/NFS compliance is enforced as work happens. Every action is logged for the FAR 4.801 contract file. It extends NCMS, SAM.gov, and ORBIT; never replaces them. It is a prototype, not an official NASA system, and must never imply endorsement.

PRINCIPLES
1. Mission first: every acquisition links to a mission and mission date; every metric is impact on that date.
2. Write once, use everywhere: facts live in one acquisition_facts row; documents read from it; nothing is retyped.
3. Regulation as data: thresholds, clauses, review routing, phase plans, template versions, and citations are database rows with effective dates. Nothing regulatory is hard-coded.
4. Every action is recorded: who, when, what changed, why.
5. Two audiences, one system: leaders see readiness and decisions; the team sees tasks, owners, dependencies, workflow.
6. Binding vs guidance: FAR, NFS, and PCDs bind; the NFS Companion Guide, PICs, and PNs are guidance. Every citation shows its tier.
7. Fictional data only: no real vendors, prices, personnel, or contract numbers. Real NASA Center names are fine.
8. Accessible by default: WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled inputs, no meaning by color alone.

ROLES
Executive: lands on the Executive Overview, read-only. Contracting Specialist/Officer: lands on the Work Queue, full workspace. Reviewer (legal, pricing, small business, IT security): reads, comments, votes Go/No-go (No-go requires a reason); reviewers never approve; approval stays with the CO and review chain. Requester: submits the NF 1707 intake, sees own status. HQ: executive plus posting rights for announcements and watch items. Roles are a header toggle backed by five seeded Supabase Auth users. RULES THE ENGINE MUST HONOR
Clause status, date, and applicability come from the two matrices, never from memory. Removed clauses never appear in a new document. FAR 52.212-5 is Reserved; never build a 52.212-5 checkbox block. Commercial buys use the SF 1449 streamlined format. The CO's signature on the SF 1449 is the affirmative responsibility determination; a memo is generated only for nonresponsibility. The PNM is the price reasonableness determination of record (FAR 12.204(b)(1)). Solicitations and contracts are built in NCMS (NFS CG 1804.11); T-Minus produces a handoff packet, not the document of record. Thresholds display their conflict note where one exists (certified cost or pricing data: $2.5M FAR text, $10M statute after June 30, 2026; JOFOC tiers pending a proposed rule). Legal and pricing review triggers are Center-configurable and say "Center policy."
