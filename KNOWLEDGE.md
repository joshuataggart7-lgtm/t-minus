# T-Minus KNOWLEDGE.md (companion to the project knowledge field)

This file holds the parts of Section A that exceed the 10,000-character knowledge field: the data model, computed metrics, and seed description. Upload it to the Lovable project alongside the t-minus-seed folder, and keep a copy at the repo root for Claude Code.

### Data model (Supabase)
- `missions`: mission_id, name, program, center_code, milestone (what the mission needs, in plain words), milestone_date (the mission need date), priority (1 is highest), program_owner, leadership_note (optional manual override for the callout).
- `acquisition_facts`: acquisition_id, mission_id, is_critical_path (this acquisition gates the milestone), title, center_code, branch_code, requester_name, requester_org_code, pr_number, description_of_requirement, estimated_value, period_of_performance_start, period_of_performance_end, place_of_performance, naics_code, psc_code, contract_type, acquisition_method, competition, set_aside, jofoc_authority_citation, commercial_determination, contract_format (SF 1449 streamlined or UCF), funding_fiscal_year, funds_certified, vendor_legal_name, vendor_uei, vendor_cage, co_name, co_code, cor_name, regulatory_baseline_date, need_date, target_award_date, lead_to_delivery_days (days from award until the mission has what it bought), clock_state (running/hold/launched/scrubbed), hold_reason, hold_owner, status, current_phase, created_at, updated_at.
- `phase_plan`: plan_id, acquisition_type (rule against acquisition_facts), phase, planned_days, order. Values: `phase_plan.csv` (derived from the Procurement LOE Estimator's timeline model, build 2026-09-09).
- `polls`: poll_id, acquisition_id, phase, reviewer_role, reviewer_name, vote (go/no-go/pending), reason, due_date, voted_at. Created automatically from `review_rules` when a phase that requires reviews begins. The phase clears when every vote is Go. A pending or No-go vote at phase exit places the acquisition on hold with the reason "awaiting [role]" or the No-go reason and sets hold_owner to that reviewer.
- `templates`: template_id, name, governing_citation, citation_tier (binding/guidance), hq_revision_date, status (live/planned), applies_when (JSON rule against acquisition_facts), html_source.
- `documents`: document_id, acquisition_id, template_id, field_values (JSON), version, ai_model, ai_generated_at, reviewed_by, reviewed_at, saved_by, saved_at.
- `sam_checks`: check_id, acquisition_id, vendor_uei, check_type (entity/exclusions), response_json, checked_by, checked_at.
- `audit_log`: log_id, acquisition_id, actor, action, field, old_value, new_value, reason, logged_at. Insert only. No update or delete policy.
- `comments`: comment_id, document_id, author, body, created_at.
- `thresholds`: threshold_id, name, value, citation, effective_date, superseded_date. Rows: micro-purchase threshold, simplified acquisition threshold, FAR 13.5 ceiling, certified cost or pricing data threshold, subcontracting plan threshold, CPARS reporting threshold, HQ Notification of Contract Action threshold, Procurement Strategy Meeting threshold. Values: `thresholds.csv`, verified on September 11, 2026 against the RFO deviated FAR text, the interim NFS, and the NFS Companion Guide, each row with its citation and tier. Two rows carry a conflict note that the interface must show: certified cost or pricing data ($2.5M in the FAR text; $10M by statute for contracts entered into after June 30, 2026) and the JOFOC approval tiers (a June 23, 2026 RFO proposed rule would realign them). Do not invent numbers.
- `review_rules`: rule_id, reviewer_role, trigger (JSON rule against acquisition_facts and thresholds), citation, planned_days. Rows: legal review, pricing review, small business specialist coordination, Procurement Strategy Meeting, HQ Notification of Contract Action (PCD 25-16), CIO review for IT under Part 39. Triggers: `review_rules.csv`, verified September 11, 2026. Legal review and pricing review have no agency-wide dollar trigger in the NFS or Companion Guide (Center policy and the forthcoming HQ Coordination and Approval Matrix govern), so those two rows are Center-configurable and display a "Center policy" label. Small business coordination (NF 1787) applies above the micro-purchase threshold. CIO authorization applies to any IT at any value. NPA applies at $7M to under $30M and ANOSCA at $30M or above or by significant interest. The aviation safety review (NPR 7900.3), Section 508 / OCIO authorization, and quality POC are driven by NF 1707 answers.
- `regulatory_refs`: ref_id, citation, title, tier (binding/guidance), source (FAR/NFS/PCD/Companion Guide/PIC/PN), effective_date, url, applies_to_phase.
- `clauses`: clause_number, title, source (FAR/NFS), prescription_citation, applies_when (JSON rule: commercial or noncommercial, contract type, thresholds, place of performance, other conditions), fill_ins (JSON), effective_date, status (active/removed/reserved), pcd_reference. Seed from three files: `clauses.csv` (the NCMS clause export of September 10, 2026, already reconciled: each row carries the disposition from the matrices), `clause_matrix_26-03B.csv` (the PCD 26-03B NASA RFO Clause Matrix, June 25, 2026: 819 FAR provisions and clauses with disposition No Change, Updated, Removed, New, or Moved, the RFO number, and the NASA post-RFO date), and `nfs_clause_matrix_2026-07-23.csv` (the NFS 1852 applicability matrix updated July 23, 2026: 125 rows with date, prescription, UCF section, fill-in and flow-down flags, the PCD that deviated each clause, and applicability by contract type: supply, R&D, service, construction, T&M/LH, A&E, IDIQ, transportation, simplified, commercial). Rules the engine must honor: clause status, date, and applicability come from the matrices, never from memory or typed by hand; the 146 clauses the 26-03B matrix marks Removed never appear in a new document; FAR 52.212-5 is Reserved under the RFO and clauses formerly bundled under it are prescribed independently, so never build a 52.212-5 checkbox block; Center-local clauses in the NCMS export (ARC, GSFC, KSC, GRC, MSFC, LaRC, JSC, NSSC, GCAT prefixes) are outside both matrices and load with status local; rows the reconciliation flagged as verify are shown with that label until a CO clears them.
- `announcements`: announcement_id, title, body, severity (info/action required/urgent), audience_roles, audience_centers, effective_from, effective_until, requires_acknowledgment, link, posted_by, posted_at. HQ posts; everyone in the audience sees a banner until acknowledged or expired.
- `announcement_acks`: announcement_id, user_id, acknowledged_at.
- `watch_items`: item_id, source (GAO bid protest / Federal Register / PCD / PIC / PN / OP notice), external_id, title, decided_or_published_date, outcome_or_type, agency, url, summary, fetched_at, tags. Filled by Edge Functions and by HQ entries.
- `users`: user_id, name, role, center_code, branch_code.
- `enterprise_strategies`: psl, name, buying_location, mandatory_vehicles, required_coordination, applies. Seed from `enterprise_strategies.csv` (Enterprise Procurement Strategies, Rev. May 20, 2026). Strategies apply above the simplified acquisition threshold (A-100).
- `centers` and `branches`: seed lists for the drill-down.
- Additional `acquisition_facts` fields: igce_attached, sow_attached, hardware_deliverable, includes_it, enterprise_psl_check (the CO's recorded determination of the closest PSL and whether it applies).

Computed metrics (never stored, recomputed on read):
- days_to_award = target_award_date minus today.
- days_to_next_decision = the earlier of the current phase's planned exit date and the earliest pending poll due date, minus today.
- forecast_award_date = today plus remaining planned days for uncompleted phases plus current hold days.
- schedule_impact_days = milestone_date minus (forecast_award_date plus lead_to_delivery_days). Positive is margin, negative is a slip.
- time_saved_days = sum over completed phases of (planned_days minus actual_days). Shown as "n days ahead of plan" or "n days behind plan."
- status: At Risk if clock_state is hold or schedule_impact_days is negative. Needs Attention if any phase is behind plan or a poll is pending with due_date within 3 days. Otherwise On Track. Launched acquisitions show Launched with time_saved_days.
- blocker = hold_reason plus hold_owner when on hold; otherwise the pending poll (role and reviewer) or the missing document and its owner for the current phase; otherwise "none."
- callout = one sentence built from the fields, no AI: "[Mission]: [blocker] since [date], owner [name]. Award forecast [n] days [before/after] [milestone]. Decision needed: [next decision] by [date]." If leadership_note is set, it replaces the generated sentence.
A mission's row on the Executive Overview reflects its critical-path acquisition. If several are critical, the one with the lowest schedule_impact_days.

### Seed data (fictional)
Seed files are provided in the folder `t-minus-seed`. Load them exactly; do not invent additional records.
- `missions.json`: five priority projects across four Centers with milestone, milestone date, program owner, and priority.
- `acquisitions.json`: twelve acquisitions. A-2027-0101 is the competed Commercial Aviation Services sample (the intake demo). A-2027-0102 is the sole-source Commercial Aviation Services sample with the JOFOC (the document and poll demo). The other ten cover every status, one hold with a named owner, two launched with time saved, and one file started before the regulatory baseline date. Every vendor and person is fictional; Center names are real.
- `thresholds.csv`, `phase_plan.csv`, `review_rules.csv`, `enterprise_strategies.csv`, `centers_branches.csv`, `templates.csv`, `regulatory_refs.csv`: table seeds. Rows marked "confirm" are values Josh still has to verify; load them as they are and show the note in the interface.
- `templates/jofoc_04-2026.md`: the JOFOC template body, field map, conditional sections, and signature tiers.
- `templates/NASA_Technical_Evaluation_Report_07-2026.docx`: the current TER template (Rev. 07/2026). Mandatory for the technical evaluation of a sole-source proposal above the simplified acquisition threshold (NFS CG 1815.45(b), FAR 15.404-4); optional at or below. Items 1 through 7 are fixed; Item 8 (resources: direct labor by task or WBS, material, other direct costs, subcontracts, each with proposed / recommended / maximum columns) is tailored, and inapplicable parts are marked N/A rather than deleted.
- `nf1707_fields.csv`: the current NF 1707 (XFA form, "Special Approvals and Affirmations of Requisitions"), 271 fields mapped to sections and subsections with the form's own text, and Center-specific subforms flagged. The intake is built from this file.
- `clauses.csv`: 1,359 rows from the NCMS clause export of September 10, 2026, reconciled against both matrices. Result: 489 updated by the RFO, 388 unchanged, 5 moved, 1 removed clause still present (52.247-26), 126 NFS clauses matched to the July 23 matrix, 8 NFS rows not in that matrix (verify), 34 FAR rows not in the 26-03B matrix (verify), and 308 Center-local clauses. 52.212-5 is absent from the export, which is correct.
- `clause_matrix_26-03B.csv` and `nfs_clause_matrix_2026-07-23.csv`: the two authoritative matrices, described under the `clauses` table.
- `orbit_prototype_fictional.html`: the ORBIT prototype with personnel names replaced by role labels.
