# JOFOC template (HQ revision 04/2026, effective 4/27/2026, NF 1098 tab 015)

Source: agency-wide template "Justification for Other than Full and Open Competition (JOFOC)," 04/2026 revision, from Josh's Drive copy. Governing citations: FAR 6.103, FAR 6.104-1, NFS CG 1806.1, NFS CG 1806.15(a). Binding tier for the numbered items (they restate FAR 6.104-1); the instruction pages are guidance.

## Known defects in the HQ template to correct in the T-Minus build (documented by Josh, Aug 2026)
- Intro cites "FAR 6.1030." The correct citation is FAR 6.103.
- Intro says the Competition in Contracting Act requires six areas; the body enumerates eleven items at FAR 6.104-1(a)(1) through (a)(11). Use eleven.
- Item 10 cites "FAR Subpart 5.2" and "synopsized." Under the RFO the notice is publicized under Subpart 5.1 (presolicitation). Use 5.1 and "publicized."
The HTML form shows the corrected text and carries the badge "HQ 04/2026 revision; three citation corrections applied by T-Minus; reported to PGPD."

## Engine field map
- Header: Center name and acronym; solicitation/contract name and number; program/project name and acquisition identifier. All from `acquisition_facts`.
- Item 1 (6.104-1(a)(1)): buying location. From `acquisition_facts.center_code`.
- Item 2 (6.104-1(a)(2)): action type (sole-source contract / new work modification / extension), contract number if any, contractor name, description. Action type is a select; contractor name from `vendor_legal_name`.
- Item 3 (6.104-1(a)(3)): description of supplies or services, mission supported, period of performance or delivery date, estimated value. From `description_of_requirement`, `mission_id`, `period_of_performance_*`, `estimated_value`.
- Item 4 (6.104-1(a)(4)): statutory authority. Select one: 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1) through (7) as implemented by FAR 6.103-1 through 6.103-7, or for FAR 12.102 procedures 41 U.S.C. 1901 or 41 U.S.C. 1903. Store in `jofoc_authority_citation`.
- Item 5 (6.104-1(a)(5)): rationale for the authority. Free text with the guidance text shown as placeholder. If authority is 6.103-2 (urgency), require the harm-to-Government rationale and the "urgency not created by delay" statement.
- Item 6 (6.104-1(a)(6)): notice published date under Subpart 5.1, or if 6.103-2, the FAR 5.101(b)(1) exemption paragraph. Conditional on authority.
- Item 7 (6.104-1(a)(7)): fair and reasonable price determination statement (standard text) plus description of the planned proposal analysis under FAR Subpart 15.4.
- Item 8 (6.104-1(a)(8)): market research conducted and results, or reasons not conducted. Free text.
- Item 9 (6.104-1(a)(9)): other facts. If 6.103-1(c)(2) follow-on: estimated duplicated cost and derivation. If 6.103-2: NFS CG 1806.12(a) quantified injury and chronology; if extending existing services, NFS CG 1806.12(b) three statements. Conditional sections.
- Item 10 (6.104-1(a)(10)): sources that expressed interest in writing, with posting and closing dates and dispositions, or the exception if no notice was required.
- Item 11 (6.104-1(a)(11)): actions to remove barriers to competition; default sentence available.
- Signature page: selected automatically by `estimated_value` against `thresholds` (JOFOC tiers, FAR 6.104-2):
  - up to $900K: Technical Representative certification; Contracting Officer certification.
  - over $900K to $20M: plus Competition Advocate approval.
  - over $20M to $150M: plus Competition Advocate concurrence; HQ OGC block when NOJMO, ESDMD, or SOMD actions require HCA approval; Head of Contracting Activity approval.
  - over $150M and all class justifications: Competition Advocate concurrence; HCA; HQ OGC; Agency Competition Advocate; Senior Procurement Executive approval.
- Post-award posting rule (FAR 6.301): redacted JOFOC posted to the GPE within 14 calendar days after award (brand name: posted with the solicitation). T-Minus creates the posting task on the roadmap when the file reaches Award. Electronic document posting checklist (NFS CG 1804.102) is a required document at that step.

## Template body (numbered items as they read in the HQ template, corrections applied)

NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION
[Center name and acronym]
JUSTIFICATION FOR OTHER THAN FULL AND OPEN COMPETITION (JOFOC)
For [Solicitation/Contract Name/Contract Number]

1. FAR 6.104-1(a)(1) – Identification of the agency and the contracting activity, and specific identification of the document as a "Justification for other than full and open competition."
This document is a justification for other than full and open competition prepared by National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) [buying location]. The procuring agency is NASA, and the contracting activity is [buying location].

2. FAR 6.104-1(a)(2) – The nature and/or description of the action being approved.
This justification provides the rationale for contracting by other than full and open competition to award a [sole-source contract to / new work modification to contract number / extension to contract number] [contract number] with [contractor name] for [description of what is being requested; for extensions include contract number, title, period of performance, and other pertinent information].

3. FAR 6.104-1(a)(3) – A description of the supplies or services required to meet the Agency's needs (including the estimated value).
[Describe in detail the supplies or services, the mission supported, the period of performance or delivery date, and the estimated dollar value.]

4. FAR 6.104-1(a)(4) – An identification of the statutory authority permitting other than full and open competition.
The statutory authority permitting other than full and open competition is 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)[1–7], as implemented by FAR 6.103-[1–7] [name of authority].
OR: The statutory authority permitting other than full and open competition for acquisitions using the procedures in FAR 12.102 is 41 U.S.C. 1901 or 41 U.S.C. 1903.

5. FAR 6.104-1(a)(5) – A demonstration that the proposed contractor's unique qualifications or the nature of the acquisition requires use of the authority cited.
The rationale supporting the use of 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)[1–7] is [reasons] as described below. [Substantiate why the contractor is the only responsible source or how the acquisition meets the authority: limited rights in data, proprietary processes, specialized labor skills, exclusive knowledge or equipment, unique capabilities, previous work. For FAR 6.103-2, describe the extent and nature of harm to the Government if the action is not executed and demonstrate the urgency was not created by delay in processing.]

6. FAR 6.104-1(a)(6) – A description of the efforts made to ensure that offers are solicited from as many potential sources as practicable, including whether a notice was or will be publicized as required by Subpart 5.1 and, if not, which exception under 5.101 applies.
A notice to the Government Point of Entry (SAM.gov) was published on [date or TBD] in accordance with FAR Subpart 5.1, informing potential sources of NASA's intent to award this sole-source [contract / modification / extension] to [contractor]. The results are summarized in Item 10.
OR (FAR 6.103-2): The contracting officer has determined in accordance with FAR 5.101(b)(1) that this action is exempt from the notice required in FAR 5.101 because unusual and compelling urgency precludes competition to the maximum extent practicable and the Government would be seriously injured if the agency complied with the publicizing and response time periods in FAR 5.101(d). [Explain the extent competition is limited and the extent maximum practicable competition was obtained, NFS CG 1806.12(a).]

7. FAR 6.104-1(a)(7) – A determination by the contracting officer that the anticipated cost to the Government will be fair and reasonable.
The Contracting Officer's signature on this document indicates that the Contracting Officer has determined that the anticipated cost to the Government will be fair and reasonable. The contractor must be required to submit a proposal to be evaluated and negotiated by the Government. Prior to execution of the contractual instrument, a proposal analysis will be performed to ensure the final agreed-to price is fair and reasonable. [Describe how proposed cost, fee, or pricing will be determined fair and reasonable per FAR Subpart 15.4.]

8. FAR 6.104-1(a)(8) – Description of the market research conducted and the results, or a statement of the reasons market research was not conducted.
[Describe the market research conducted (request for information, market knowledge, technical journals, interchange meetings, internet resources) or why it was not performed. Reliance on the FAR 5.101 notice alone is not sufficient; demonstrate market surveillance under FAR Part 10.]

9. FAR 6.104-1(a)(9) – Any other facts supporting the use of other than full and open competition.
[Additional facts, or "none." When FAR 6.103-1(c)(2) is cited for follow-on acquisitions: estimated cost of duplicated work and how it was derived. When FAR 6.103-2 is cited: per NFS CG 1806.12(a), quantify the serious injury with the basis of the estimate, describe any probable personal injury or loss of life and why no other action could avert it, and give a chronological explanation of the events that caused the urgency; if extending existing services, per NFS CG 1806.12(b), document that the successor acquisition was started early enough, the circumstances that prevented timely award, and why no other source could compete for the interim requirement.]

10. FAR 6.104-1(a)(10) – A listing of the sources, if any, that expressed an interest in writing in the acquisition.
A notice of NASA's intent to award this sole-source action was publicized on SAM.gov under FAR Subpart 5.1 (see Item 6). [Posting and closing dates, companies that expressed interest in writing, responses received and their disposition, or a statement that none responded. If a notice was not required, describe the exception and why it applies.]

11. FAR 6.104-1(a)(11) – A statement of actions, if any, the agency may take to remove or overcome any barriers to competition before any subsequent acquisition for the supplies or services required.
[Actions to enable future competition.] Default: The Agency will continue to examine the market in the future for alternative solutions or new sources before executing any subsequent acquisitions for the same requirements.

Signature pages: see the engine field map above. Technical Representative certification: "I certify that the facts presented in this justification are accurate and complete." Contracting Officer certification: "I hereby certify that the above justification is accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge and belief."
